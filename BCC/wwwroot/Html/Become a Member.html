BECOME A MEMBER

Thank you so much for considering membership in our club. We look forward to welcoming you to our club.

Any person may join our Photography Club. All members may participate in the monthly competition.

There are no conditions for participation in the monthly competition. Although it is preferred that members attend the monthly gathering (competition), it is not a requirement for participation.

Membership fees are payable annually in advance. A joining fee is payable by new members.

Note that the club's fiscal year runs from November to October.

Photo entries of prospective members will only be accepted after payment of your membership fee.
