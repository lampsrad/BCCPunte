About

Email: admin@bkk.co.za

This website is for all people with an interest and passion for photography in Bloemfontein, the Free Sate and Northern Cape.

Our aim is to develop a platform for local enthusiasts to engage and at the same time showcase our club, our members photos and most importantly to inform people about the Bloemfontein Camera Club with the hope that it will inspire more people to attend a club meeting and enjoy it so much that they end up joining.

You are also welcome to advertise personal photographic equipment for sale or wanted and events or workshops to do with photography in our area.
